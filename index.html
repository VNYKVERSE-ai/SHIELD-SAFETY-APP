IMPORTANT

What SHIELD can and cannot do

  • GPS: May provide coordinates without mobile internet, if device location is enabled and permission is granted.
  • Remote alerts: Need SMS, mobile data, Wi-Fi, satellite service or another working communication path.
  • SMS: This prototype opens your messaging app with a prepared draft. You must review and send it.
  • 112: The call link opens your phone dialer. Confirm the call only in a genuine emergency.
  • Offline: Local screens and saved records work offline after first load. Offline storage cannot deliver a remote alert.
  • Background: This PWA does not provide reliable background tracking, automatic SMS, or Bluetooth mesh relay.
